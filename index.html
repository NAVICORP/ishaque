    2. Home, Blathur

      Just me and PowerPoint.

      From home I started taking presentation work on Fiverr: pitch decks, company profiles, anything with slides. Clients came back, then clients from abroad.

    How I work

    White space isn't empty. It's where the message lands.

    1. ListenThe audience, the goal, the deadline.
    2. Find the one thingWhat the room should believe at the end.
    3. Write the storySlide by slide, before any design.
    4. Structure itOne job per slide, in the order questions get asked.
    5. Design itType, data and imagery that read in a second.
    6. Hand it overEditable files your team can keep using.
