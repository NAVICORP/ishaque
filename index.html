The journey

The road so far.

Six stops from Calicut to Okhla and back home to Kannur. Tap through them, or swipe the map.

  1. University of Calicut

    BA, Kerala

    I studied Arabic, not design.

    A BA in Arabic at the University of Calicut. Languages first; the slides came later.

  2. Home, Kannur

    2020, Kerala

    Just me and PowerPoint.

    From home I started taking presentation work on Fiverr: pitch decks, company profiles, anything with slides. Clients came back, then clients from abroad.

  3. Jamia Millia Islamia

    MA, Okhla, New Delhi

    Off to Delhi for a master's.

    An MA in Arabic at Jamia Millia Islamia. The next stop turned out to be just down the road in Okhla.

  4. Value360 Communications

    2023 to 2025, Okhla, New Delhi

    Two years of nine to five.

    As a Senior Presentation Designer at Value360 I made decks for brands like IndiGo and Toyota Kirloskar, and learned how companies pitch to clients and decision makers.

  5. Fiverr Vetted Pro

    2025, Kannur, Kerala

    Vetted, twice.

    Fiverr made me a Vetted Pro for presentation design, then for pitch decks too.

  6. SkiFi studio

    2025, Kannur, Kerala

    More than a freelancer.

    Back home I built SkiFi into a team: presentations first, then brands, brochures, websites, UI, AI creative and video.
